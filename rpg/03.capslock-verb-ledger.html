PART 1

Three Verbs, No Numbers

Pick three. When a prompt turns risky, one of these is what you reach for — no stat behind it, just a description of what your character does under pressure.

01

HACK

Bypassing firewalls, manipulating networks, overriding drone logic.

02

PROWL

Moving unseen, tailing targets, lifting data-chits.

03

COAX

Fast-talking handlers, bribing informants, quiet intimidation.

04

SCAN

Reading digital footprints, working a scene, piecing together clues.

05

BRAWL

Street violence, desperate shootouts, breaking through what won't move.

When an action clearly matches one of your three, a "Yes, but" upgrades to a clean "Yes" — the same trick Style already pulls. Both can trigger on the same draw.

PART 1, CONTINUED

Standing

One word, not a tally. It only ever holds one of three states — and it only ever moves forward.

START HERE

STEADY

Add a flavor word or two if you like — Calculated, Wired, Connected. Texture only, nothing to track.

FIRST COST

CRACKING

Something specific and visible changes — Bleeding, Glitching, Paranoid, Burned. Pick the word that fits the scene.

SECOND COST

BREAKING

Your next entry must contain a catastrophic failure or a narrow, costly escape. No choice about it now.

Reading the Clock Draw Directly

No difficulty to set here — just "what happens next." Pick your Verb, glance at a clock, read the second straight off the bands.

BandSecondsResult
1:00–:19Clean success. You get exactly what you wanted.
2:20–:39Success with a cost. You get it — but Standing ratchets forward one stage.
3:40–:59Failure. Standing ratchets forward, and the obstacle remains or mutates.

PART 2, CONTINUED

Seven Entries

DAY 1 — THE COLD DROP

3 AM, a burner phone, coordinates to a dead drop in the transit district. Inside: an encrypted data-chit and a stack of cred-sticks.

Draw on SCAN or HACK. The security proxy plays like a Watcher — it won't fight you, it'll just lock you out and remember you were there. What did you decode first?

DAY 2 — THE WET WORK

The chit belongs to an Aisuru Biotech executive, missing 48 hours. Their signature leads to a blood-slicked alley behind a noodle shop.

Draw on PROWL to work the scene unnoticed, or BRAWL if the clean-up drones — Enforcers — spot you first. What clue ties this to the arcologies above?

DAY 3 — THE GLITCH IN THE MIRROR

Your implants glitch. Unfamiliar code loops at the bottom of your HUD. The chit is a beacon, and it's frying your rig.

Draw on COAX to get a ripperdoc to look at you off the books — a Corporate Paywall — or HACK to purge it yourself. What did the code change about what you remember?

DAY 4 — INTO THE UNDERBELLY

The clue leads to The Electric Velvet, hunting a broker called Zero-Day.

Draw on PROWL past biometric security or COAX your way into the VIP lounge. What does Zero-Day reveal about who actually hired you?

DAY 5 — THE FRAME-UP

Sirens. Your face is on the billboards. You've been framed. Accounts frozen, apartment compromised — your Safe Zone stops being safe. Cross it off.

Draw on PROWL past a patrol drone or BRAWL through a checkpoint. Who just stopped answering your calls?

DAY 6 — THE SYSTEM CRASH

The unencrypted files reveal the plan: a predatory neural virus, field-tested on the city's lower tiers. The array traces to a server farm — a Hunter, no clean fight available.

Draw on HACK to breach the matrix or BRAWL to breach the floor. As the alarms scream — save yourself, or expose the corp?

DAY 7 — THE FINAL LOG

Rain outside the window, or inside the wreck of the server room. Check your Standing.

Breaking → write a noir ending — tragic sacrifice, or cynical survival. Cracking or Steady → you came through intact. How do you use what you know now that the city knows your name?
