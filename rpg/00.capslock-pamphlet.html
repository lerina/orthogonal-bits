WHAT IT IS

Everything Fits In Your Skull

CAPSLOCK is a story you carry through a commute, a chore, a long walk at night. There is nothing to print, nothing to shuffle, nothing to lose in a bag. One character, one city, one voice in your head arguing with itself.

It's built for the in-between moments — the drive home, the line at the noodle stand, the fifteen minutes before you fall asleep. You don't sit down to play CAPSLOCK. You just start narrating, and the city fills in around you.

THE LORE

One Corp. One Gang. One Bar.

The city doesn't need a hundred factions to feel alive. It needs three, and a rule for the rest: whenever something in this world doesn't make sense, ask who profits — and the answer writes the lore for you on the spot.

THE MEGACORP

Faceless, city-spanning, unaccountable. It doesn't hate you. It doesn't know you exist. That's worse.

THE STREET GANG

The threat with a face and a name, three blocks from wherever you're standing. Loud, local, personal.

THE SAFE ZONE

One door that locks from the inside. A noodle bar, a safehouse, a name only your crew knows.

THE PEOPLE IN IT

Doesn't kick doors down. Doesn't need to. By the time anyone notices the terminal's been touched, she's three blocks gone and the alarm hasn't caught up to her yet.

WHAT HUNTS YOU

The City Bites Back

Nothing in this city has a health bar. It has a temperament.

WATCHERS

Cameras. Drones. Informants.

Never fight you directly. Only ever tell someone worse that you're here.

ENFORCERS

Guards. Gangers. Turrets.

Stand between you and the door, and mean it.

HUNTERS

Solos. Netrunners. Mechs.

You don't beat these head-on. You outthink them, or you run.

TONIGHT'S TROUBLE

Pick a Way In

THE BROKEN UPLINK

You're wedged in a maintenance shaft two floors below a Megacorp server farm, a stolen drive cooking your neural port, and one patrol drone standing between you and the exit.

THE BLACK MARKET DROP

A prototype cyber-eye, a crowded noodle bar, and a message across your vision that reads: they followed me — don't look up.

THE NEON CHASE

A stolen flying car, an autopilot that hates you personally, and a skyline full of gaps just narrow enough to be a problem.

C A P S L O C K

No book to buy. No character sheet to fill out. Just a city, three names, and however far you can walk before the story runs out of road.
