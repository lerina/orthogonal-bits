What's Different in This Edition

The base CAPSLOCK rulebook holds itself to the Rule of Three on purpose — three Gear Slots, two Cyberware Tags, one Style — so a whole character fits in a single breath. Extended Edition lifts that ceiling. It's for players who want more surface area: fuller character generation, oracle tables for building a premise or an alter-ego from scratch, and a real-life progression layer. Everything still resolves off the Digit-Sum Draw — nothing here brings dice back.
