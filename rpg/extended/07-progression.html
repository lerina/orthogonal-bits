CHAPTER 07

PROGRESSION

The base game keeps this to one number on purpose. Now that the ceiling's lifted, here's the fuller version for players who actually want to watch the trend line.

OPTIONAL BY DESIGN

Why This Is Optional

Every other side text in this system stops at a single Streak counter, because tracking more than that fights the base game's "fits in your head" philosophy. Extended Edition has already opened that door once, in Chapter 02 — so here's the fuller layer for anyone using CAPSLOCK primarily as a real-life gamification tool, where watching a trend actually matters. If it starts feeling like a chore, drop straight back to a single Streak number. Nothing else in the system depends on this chapter.

WHAT GETS TRACKED

Three Numbers

XP

Skcor completed per 5 days

A Skcor is one 20-minute block of real, focused deep work. Xp is how many you've banked this 5-day stretch.

HP

Consistency streak

Every 5 days without breaking the chain, fold that period's Skcor into your total and add 1 to Hp. Reset Xp, start the next stretch.

LV

Real accomplishments

Increments only for something with real consequences to your life — a license, a shipped project, a finished course. Not busywork.

Optional: note the stretch number and total Skcor alongside each Hp increment — a running log of how your deep-work capacity is trending, not just whether you kept the chain.
CONNECTING THE CHAPTERS

Where the Numbers Come From

A completed Nine-Item Loadout day (Chapter 05) is a natural place to log Skcor — any of the nine items that involved real focused effort counts. A completed Story Arc (Chapter 04) is a natural place to check whether an Lv-worthy accomplishment happened along the way.

Lesser and Major Awards: before starting a Story Arc, define two rewards for finishing it well — a Lesser Award for a clean run, a Major Award for finishing in a way that actually surprised you. Cool gear, a new title, a rare piece of Capability — whatever lands. Decide both before you start; awarding them after the fact turns them into participation trophies instead of goals.
