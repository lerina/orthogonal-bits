CHAPTER 05

REAL-LIFE MISSIONS

Quest Deconstruction (core rulebook, Section 12) turns one target into one quest. This chapter scales that up to a full day — nine items, mapped straight onto the story.

THE DAILY STRUCTURE

The Nine-Item Loadout

Instead of deconstructing one target at a time, pick nine for the day, no filtering — write them all down first, sort after:

3 TO DO

Actions

Things you'll actively do today.

3 NOT TO DO

Restraints

Things you'll deliberately avoid today.

3 TO COMPLETE

Tasks

Things you'll finish today.

The character's story only moves forward as far as the day actually went. Run each of the nine through Quest Deconstruction in miniature — a Target and a First Move is enough; you don't need all six steps for all nine. Hitting all nine is itself the day's Reward: one scene's worth of story, unlocked.

TRANSLATION

Mapping Real Life to the Scene

Match each real item to something happening in the current Story Arc (Chapter 04). Continuing the datacenter-rescue example:

One day's loadout, mapped onto the ongoing arc

Not every real item needs a dramatic translation. A dull chore that maps to nothing is fine — not every day of a real campaign is a set piece either.
WHEN COMPLETION IS FUZZY

Half-Finished Items

Some days an item gets started but not finished clean. Rather than a binary check, run it through a Digit-Sum Draw (Chapter 01) and let the six-outcome table decide what the scene actually looked like:

Example: laundry got half-done before you gave up. Draw comes back "Yes, but" — the loot got split, but someone's owed a favor over it now. The scene still moves forward; it just isn't clean.
