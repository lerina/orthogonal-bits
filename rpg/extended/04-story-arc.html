CHAPTER 04

THE STORY ARC — CIRCLE & KISHOTENKETSU

PICO·ATTO shapes one scene. This chapter shapes everything above it — a premise stretched across a whole run, phase by phase, in two sizes.

ZOOMING OUT

A Shape Bigger Than a Scene

The core rulebook's PICO·ATTO backbone (Section 11) answers "what happens in this scene." It was never meant to answer "when does the whole story end." This chapter adds that missing layer — an arc that a generated Premise (Chapter 03) can grow into across several sessions, with PICO·ATTO still doing the work inside each individual phase.

THE LONG FORM

The Eight-Phase Story Circle

One phase per major session or milestone. Move through them in order — nothing skips ahead.

1

YOU

The character in their comfort zone, before anything starts.

2

NEED

Something is wanted. The inciting incident — often the Chapter 03 Premise landing on them.

3

GO

They commit and step into unfamiliar territory.

4

SEARCH

They adapt to the new situation — scouting, gathering, learning the shape of the problem.

5

FIND

They get what they came for. The midpoint.

6

TAKE

And pay a price for it. Something is lost in the same move that wins something.

7

CHANGE

They become capable of something they weren't before. The climax.

8

RETURN

Back to where they started — changed. Loose ends tied, ready for the next Premise.

THE SHORT FORM

Kishotenketsu — The Four-Phase Compression

Eight phases is a lot to hold across a long run. Compress to four when a shorter arc fits better:

Each compressed phase folds two circle phases together
PhaseMeaningFolds together
KiIntroductionYou + Need
ShoDevelopmentGo + Search
TenTwistFind + Take — the real climax, wherever it lands
KetsuConclusionChange + Return

Either version answers the one question the base rulebook leaves open: when does this story actually end, and what does "done" feel like.

CONTINUED FROM CHAPTER 03

Running the Circle on the Datacenter Job

Same premise as the Chapter 03 worked example: an old mentor asks for a rescue, for revenge, based on a tip from someone once trusted, across a stretch of open city.

YOU

Freelance by day, ghost by night — good at getting things done quietly, better at making things happen when nobody's looking.

NEED

The mentor calls an old, informal meeting. The mission: an extraction under duress. The incentive surfaces fast — the people holding the target are the same ones who wiped out everyone this character used to run with.

GO

The job is accepted. First scenes push the character out of the Safe Zone and into the open stretch of city the Complication warned about.

SEARCH → FIND → TAKE

Scouting the datacenter's approach (Search), reaching the target inside (Find), and paying for it — the old acquaintance who supplied the tip turns out to be compromised (Take).

CHANGE → RETURN

The rescue succeeds, but revenge and rescue turn out to want different endings — the character has to choose which one this was really about (Change), before heading back to the Safe Zone changed by the answer (Return).
